| Row | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 4-D1 | **Branch.** `node/ev1-4` from `node/ev1-3b` 8767592f2 (EV1-3b's head). Local, signed, not pushed. EV1-3b and EV1-5a follow-merge once both have merged | manager brief |
| 4-D2 | **Scope.** `dash_dml::specialtx::provider` ports `CheckProUpServTx` (`specialtxman.cpp:1383-1486`) and `CheckProUpRevTx` (:1590-1621) in Core's order; the dispatcher's `Unported` arms for types 2 and 4 are gone. | 4-D4 | **Legacy-flag cases in L1 (design D30, A-4).** The pinned dash-pkc verifies under the legacy scheme, so L1 runs `check_sigs = true` cases with a legacy global flag (worlds before V19, `FLAG_LEGACY`) instead of excluding them, and counts them apart: `Case::legacy_flag`, and `node-l1/examples/specialtx_census.rs`, which counts each operator BLS verification (a ProUpServTx or ProUpRevTx in a world that checks signatures, accepted or refused `bad-protx-sig`) under the flag it runs under, and the cases where Core's verdict and ours differ (Opus r1 m-2).
