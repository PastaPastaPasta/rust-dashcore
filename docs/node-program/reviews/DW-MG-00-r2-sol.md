# MG-00 r2 independent design review

Reviewed `65ced72f..173c59bc` in `dw/mg-00-ux-spec`, the changed light and dark PNG frames, Dash Core's special-transaction filtering, registration and payment code, the parked governance tally, rust-dashcore's simplified-list entry, and the pinned Platform wallet at `ebe37f8a`. I assume the desktop uses the specified SPV and peer-synced governance sources rather than a full node. The manager's D1, D26, D28 and D19 decisions stand.

## Findings

1. **Major — The collateral backfill does not say how to derive an internal collateral outpoint.** `docs/design/UX-SPEC.md:980-984`; `docs/design/MG-00-masternodes-ux.md:141,152` (D43, MG-03). The block lookup is sound: Core's `specialtxman.cpp:331-342` sets `confirmedHash` to the block at registered height + `nMasternodeMinimumConfirmations`, one block before the list records confirmation. The wallet can find that hash's height in its verified header chain, subtract 15 on mainnet or 1 on testnet, then use the header at the resulting height to check the downloaded registration block. However, the text says the found ProRegTx is “with the collateral outpoint.” A FundNew or shared ProRegTx has a **null hash** in its payload outpoint; Core constructs the real outpoint as `(ProRegTx txid, payload collateralOutpoint.n)` (`specialtxman.cpp:1196-1214`). Copying the payload outpoint leaves common tracked nodes unable to vote. State both cases explicitly, validate the indexed output's amount and type, and use the derived outpoint for D43 and the vote mapping.

2. **Major — The unconfirmed withdrawal recovery contract cannot be implemented through the pinned wallet API, and nonce advancement alone does not prove success.** `docs/design/UX-SPEC.md:1019-1024`; `docs/design/MG-00-masternodes-ux.md:142,164` (D44, MG-13). At `ebe37f8a`, `masternode_withdrawal.rs:464-518` signs and broadcasts inside `masternode_withdraw`, while `MasternodeWithdrawalUnconfirmed` exposes only identity ID, amount and reason (`error.rs:935-944`), not the nonce, transition ID or signed bytes. The caller therefore cannot persist or rebroadcast the **same** signed transition as specified. Also, a nonce past the attempt could have been consumed by another device's transition; it is not proof that this withdrawal landed. Require an extended Platform-wallet API that persists the signed transition/ID and nonce before broadcast, returns them with ambiguous outcomes, and supports lookup and identical rebroadcast. Mark “Withdrawal done” only from proof of that transition (or an equivalent uniquely attributable record); treat an advanced nonce with no match as a conflict that stays unresolved.

3. **Major — The advertised numeric governance error bound is not a bound on the peer-synced result.** `docs/design/UX-SPEC.md:1052-1078`; `docs/design/MG-00-masternodes-ux.md:143,151` (D45, MG-02). A conservative range for the weights of *received* votes with unverified outpoints can be calculated from the list (a vote might count zero, one or four). SPV peers do not prove that they supplied every latest vote, however. An omitted or superseded vote can change a tally even when every received vote's outpoint is verified. Thus “off by up to 12: not enough to change this result,” the exact-count rule based solely on zero unverified weight, and “Your masternodes” exact current votes overstate what the source proves. Specify the range as conditional on the peer-seen vote set, label own votes “last vote seen” where appropriate, and remove claims that the absolute funding result cannot change unless vote-set completeness is established. Keep the neutral chip when the defensible range crosses the line or budget order.

4. **Major — The shared reward copy assumes an operator payout is active before it can be.** `docs/design/UX-SPEC.md:1337-1343,1355-1359`; `docs/design/MG-00-masternodes-ux.md:144,154`; frames `sh-join`, `sh-session`, `sh-detail` (D46). The 30% × 95% = 28.5% arithmetic is correct **when** the operator payout script is set. Core's `payments.cpp:162-179` subtracts the operator's 5% only when that script is nonempty; ProRegTx has no operator payout script, and ProUpServTx sets it later. Yet the invitation and locked terms promise about 28.5% of each payout and say Alice “takes” 5%, while §4.25 itself says owners get their full shares until the service update. MG-03's follow-up applies ProUpRegTx/ProUpShareTx but does not track the ProUpServTx needed to know that state, although `specialtx_filter.cpp:89-96` matches it by ProTx hash. Track and reconcile ProUpServTx as well, and make the invitation, locked terms and detail pane state both the current 30% share and the conditional 28.5% after the operator payout is set.

5. **Minor — The governance mock leaves estimated components looking exact.** `docs/design/ux/mg-00/src/governance.html:58-59`; frame `gov-list`. The pane labels net yes as “about 771” and says the estimate can be off by 12, but its legend says “Yes 812 · No 41 · Abstain 9” without an estimate marker. D45 requires estimated counts to read “about.” Mark the affected legend values as estimates or show their ranges, then regenerate both theme PNGs.

6. **Minor — The pending-withdrawal frame still advertises credits ready to withdraw.** `docs/design/ux/mg-00/src/masternodes.html:20`; frame `mn-withdraw-pending` (light and dark). The dimmed summary says “0.42 ready to withdraw” while the modal says a withdrawal is pending and Withdraw is disabled. §4.25.2:1025 explicitly calls for “withdrawal being checked” in that summary. Render the pending summary and evonode pane behind this modal. The full D12 privacy warning is appropriate and needs no change.

## Verified closures and limits

- D42's ProTx-hash scan matches all relevant update types: Core's `specialtx_filter.cpp:89-146` inserts the hash for ProUpServTx, ProUpRegTx, ProUpShareTx and ProUpSharedRegTx. The “Checking…” gate prevents a stale owner payout from being shown as current while reconciliation runs.
- D41 can preserve the exact 1,000 Dash collateral output: the mock reserves 1,000.0003 Dash in aggregate; a 0.00006 Dash fee and 0.00024 Dash of change leave exactly 1,000 Dash as collateral. Its per-participant fee parts sum to the total and stay below the stated maxima.
- D24's custody variants, D19's estimated-cutoff wording, D16's one-versus-six-confirmation wording, and the restored D12 warning close the corresponding r1 findings. D43, D44, D45 and D46 still need the corrections above.
